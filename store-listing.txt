=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (Chrome Web Store summary, max 132 characters) ===

Take control of Odoo chatter — hide, resize & act in one click. Pins, debug mode & recents included. Free, private, open source.


=== FULL DESCRIPTION (Chrome Web Store) ===

Stop fighting Odoo’s chatter panel. Get the screen space you need, the layout you prefer, and the shortcuts that save you time — every single day.

Odoo Chatter Utilities is built for consultants, developers, and power users who live in Odoo all day. One extension. Less clutter. More focus.

Designed for Odoo 17, 18 and 19. Native invoice/PDF and bottom chatter layouts keep their own placement and sizing.

✨ Why users love it

🔘 Hide & show chatter in a second
Collapse the panel when you need room to think. Bring it back instantly — from the chatter bar, a sleek side rail, or a keyboard shortcut (Alt+Shift+C / ⌘⇧X). Your call, your workflow.

↔️ Your perfect split, every time
Drag the divider between form and chatter until it feels right. Too narrow? It folds away on its own. Come back later — it remembers exactly how you like it.

⚡ Act without reopening chatter
Chatter hidden but need to log a note, send a message, ping WhatsApp, or schedule an activity? Do it straight from the folded icon rail. No extra clicks. No context switching.

📐 Layout that fits how you work
Keep chatter on the side for classic CRM-style forms, or stack it below when that’s clearer. Switch once in settings — stay productive.

📏 Reclaim the full form when you need it
Optional stretch mode gives your form the full width when chatter is out of the way — perfect for dense records, wide tables, and focused data entry.

🛠️ Debug mode that actually stays on
Turn on permanent debug once and forget about it. No more losing ?debug=1 every time Odoo navigates somewhere new. Built for implementers who debug for a living.

🕓 Never lose your place
Jump back to recently opened records in one click. Pin the pages you hit ten times a day. Spend less time hunting, more time doing.

🌐 Works on your Odoo — not just odoo.com
Self-hosted or custom domain? Grant access only to the instances you use. You stay in control.

⚡ Settings in one click
Jump straight to Odoo’s Settings app from the extension popup. Small thing. Big time-saver.

🎨 Support pages, upgraded (Odoo employees)
Cleaner support documentation with styling and navigation enhancements for internal teams.
